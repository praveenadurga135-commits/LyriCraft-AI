# 🎵 LyriCraft

### Your AI-Powered Songwriting Companion

LyriCraft is an AI-powered songwriting platform designed for independent musicians, lyricists, and creative artists. It transforms a simple idea, mood, genre, and theme into a structured song while helping users refine their lyrics through rhyme analysis, syllable counting, and musical mood-board suggestions.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Independent musicians and songwriters often struggle with creative blocks and the difficulty of turning an initial idea into a complete, structured song.

LyriCraft provides an intelligent songwriting companion that helps users:

- Generate original song lyrics from creative ideas
- Choose suitable song structures
- Analyze rhyme schemes
- Count syllables and evaluate lyrical flow
- Suggest instrumentation and tempo
- Refine and regenerate individual sections
- Save and revisit previously created songs

The goal is to provide artists with a fast and creative starting point while keeping the songwriter in control of the final composition.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎼 AI Lyrics Generation

Users can provide:

- Mood
- Musical genre
- Song theme or narrative
- Desired song structure

LyriCraft generates structured lyrics with sections such as:

- Verse
- Chorus
- Bridge
- Intro
- Outro

---

### 🎤 Song Structure Suggestions

Users can choose from predefined structures such as:

- Classic Pop
- Standard Radio / Indie
- Storyteller / Folk
- Epic / Arena Rock
- Songwriter DIY
- Custom Structure

This helps users create lyrics with a natural musical progression.

---

### 🔤 Rhyme Scheme Analysis

LyriCraft analyzes the ending words of lyric lines and identifies rhyme patterns such as:

- AABB
- ABAB
- AAB
- Other rhyme patterns

It also provides rhyme suggestions to help songwriters refine their lyrics.

---

### 🔢 Syllable & Meter Analysis

The platform analyzes:

- Syllables per line
- Average syllables per line
- Section-level syllable patterns
- Meter consistency

This helps songwriters create lyrics that are easier to sing and fit a consistent rhythm.

---

- Recommended tempo/BPM
- Instrumentation
- Tone and aesthetics
- Production vibe
- Mood descriptors
